the actual site

harumakigohan.com · its real section headers

Pulled from the live site on 2026-08-22, from wp-content/themes/gohantheme/img/. Two measured facts about that page first: it has 124 images and ZERO html headings - not one h1 through h6 anywhere - and not a single tilde character in its text. Every section header is a drawn PNG. The m_ prefix is midashi, Japanese for heading.

The structure is the same in all four: a drawn squiggle on the left, the white label, a drawn squiggle on the right. Colour census over the pixels gives #f0f0f0 for the label and #f078f0 for the ornaments, a bright magenta-pink. Heights are a fixed 30px on the main sections and 22px on the smaller ones; width just follows the word.

What this means for C, and I nearly got it backwards. I was about to report the "harumaki ~tilde~" citation as fabricated, because there is no tilde on their site. It is not fabricated - it is an approximation. Their flanking ornament is real and it is exactly the device C copies; what C does is substitute a typed ~ for a drawn wave, and gold for their pink. So the idea has a real parent, and the execution is a text stand-in for artwork.

That is D10 in one artefact: the warmth is in the drawn asset. Matching this properly means drawing an ornament, not typing a character - and per D10 that is shader and asset work, not a CSS problem.
