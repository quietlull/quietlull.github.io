real geometry, procedural hand · lab · clean set
How the old ones were actually made: they were AI-drawn approximations. draw-in-icons.css says so in its own header - "icon GEOMETRY is the hand-approximated placeholder set relabeled with Rod's tools, official SVGs owed". Nothing was traced. The cartoony quality was a failure mode that happened to look handmade.

What is below instead: the real official marks, byte-identical, with the wobble added as a procedural filter. Same look, honest provenance, and it lands on D10's side - the handmade signature being shader work rather than drawn art.

feTurbulence makes fractal noise, feDisplacementMap pushes the stroke around with it. No library, no redrawn paths. It is the same family of technique the scene's paper filter already uses.

4 · Boiling — mid, re-rolled 3.25 times a second

The seed steps discretely rather than sweeping, so the line JUMPS between drawings the way hand-drawn animation boils, instead of sliding around like jelly. 3.25Hz is not a new number - it is CONFIG.lanterns.paper.boilHz, so the icons breathe at exactly the rate the scene's paper already does.
