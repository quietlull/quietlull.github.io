the proposals were A/B-measured for real - what actually moves the needle

"can you do agents to see how big of a change these changes would actually be on performance"

Each option was applied AT RUNTIME to the live page (exact same shader code as the diffs, injected over CDP - no repo files touched) and measured against a same-load baseline under software rendering, at 1x and with a 4x CPU throttle. The decisive result: the RIPPLE GATE alone is most of the whole win (+2.6 to +4.9 ms depending on the run; it returns the dock to a 60 fps typical frame at full speed but NOT on the throttled proxy - see the corrected row above, which supersedes this line). Burst frame-drops are killed by EITHER shells-out-of-reflection OR trail 10 to 5 (p95 33.3 to 16.8 both; trail 7 does NOT clear it). Cheap-bleed is the best bloom cut (+1.25 throttled). Two honest negatives: the far-water octave drop measured nothing, and the weak-machine preset AS FIRST SPECCED measured nothing at the dock (its buffer shrinks miss the real costs) - the preset doc now carries the revised, measured recipe. Whole recommended stack: +5.6 ms throttled, typical dock frame back at 16.7. Measured blocks are at the top of each perf-diffs doc.
