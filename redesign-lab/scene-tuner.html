Bloom (Dual Kawase, 2 levels)

NOT a blur width. It rebalances the tight band against the wide one, and widens the tap reach from 0.25 to 1.0 source texels across its range.
Depth is fixed at 2 levels and there is no threshold: the bright pass is gone, so the whole frame blurs and is added back (D23). Tone mapping is gone too - the scene is lit by the sky now, not by an output curve.

Paper filter (APPROVED 2026-08-22)

Folded into the bloom composite, so it adds NO pass and NO render target. Two sheets cost two texture fetches and ONE extra tap of the frame - the normals are combined before the tap, not sampled twice. This is what pays for dpr 0.5 / bloom 0.25 / reflection 0.25.
Repeats across the screen. Higher = finer grain.
Real paper has structure at more than one scale. Put the coarse sheet in A at a low tile and the fine one in B at a high tile.
Both sheets are SIGNED fields (normal in -1..1, height centred on 0), so these are signal ops, not Photoshop modes. Add and Subtract can push past the normal range - that is allowed, it just means mix wants to come down.
Lerp: 0 = sheet A only, 1 = sheet B only. Every other mode: how hard B acts on A.
Master. Scales bleed and tooth together.
How much of the image comes from the tap pulled along the paper normal. The source blended 0.4/0.6.
The sheet's own surface, added OVER the glow so the scene reads as printed on it. This is the knob that decides whether it looks like paper or like fog.
Re-rolls the sheets N times a second instead of every frame - the hand-drawn "boiling line". Range narrowed to 1.5-5 by Rod; the checkbox above is the only off switch now. The two sheets boil out of step so they do not pulse together.

Sky & water (the water reflects the sky)
