current vs the blockout ramp · alpha held at .55 so only hue changes ← lab index
Your ask, P53: "change the card colors to a grey similar to the current blockout colors." The change is one line — --color-panel in extracted/styles/settings.css — but that line repaints every card on every page including the landing, which is built out and 8-of-11 approved. So it is drawn rather than committed, and "similar to" gets a number.

What is actually blue right now: --color-panel: rgba(12, 16, 38, 0.55). That is 38 blue against 12 red — a real hue, not a neutral. --color-panel-solid and foundations.css --panel are both #161d39, the same colour twice under two names.

Every candidate below holds alpha at .55, so the card stays translucent per D27 and nothing about the box moves. Only the hue changes.

Three tokens are deliberately not in this set: --color-stamp-paper (#1e160e, your own stamp callout, whose comment says the dots must match exactly), --color-glass (the top bar's backdrop, chrome not a card) and --color-surface (already a neutral white wash with no hue at all).

The thing worth deciding, not just the swatch. Two of the candidates separate a card from its ground by going lighter (#282828), and two by going darker (#141414, dimden). The blockout's own answer is neither — #1b1b1b on #141414 is a 7-point step, which is MinionsArt's trick exactly: "contrast with the black parent is tiny, so the box separates by fill alone." On the night ground that reads fine. On the projects panel, where a card sits inside a card, a 7-point step may vanish. That is why both grounds are drawn.

When you pick, the change is:
extracted/styles/settings.css:27  --color-panel: rgba(12, 16, 38, 0.55) → rgba(NN, NN, NN, 0.55)
extracted/styles/settings.css:28  --color-panel-solid: #161d39 → #NNNNNN
foundations.css:6  --panel: #161D39 → #NNNNNN  (the same colour under a second name)

Flagging one consequence rather than deciding it: the cards going neutral removes the last blue from every surface except the sky. T0-A ("warm + one cool") already got overturned by D27 when the tape took the colour; this finishes that move. If the blue in the cards was doing work you wanted kept, this is the moment to say so.
