Something solid to be in the way

This card is opaque. That matters: the glow ships at z-index: -1, which puts it behind every solid thing on the page, so over a card like this one it is simply not there. Move the layer dial and watch it arrive on top.

The sparkler has the opposite problem and always has — its canvas is already at 500, above everything on every final page. That is the number the glow is being measured against. There is a link and some buttons below because the sparkler samples the warm colour under the cursor and those are the elements it looks for.

Reading weight

Body copy at the weight the site actually uses, so the wash can be judged for whether it fights the text. A glow that is beautiful over empty ground and unreadable over a paragraph is not tuned, it is just pretty in one place.

Provenance, stated not settled

cursor-glow is Remixed / theirs, and it is not one of the circular-citation components. element-tracker.md:45 credits brittanychiang.com (#about cursor glow) and merodev for the fixed radial at --cx/--cy, recoloured purple to gold, and records Rod authorising it as not-slop on 2026-06-09.

The P380 row says it is "one of the 20 components whose files mention circular". That is wrong. Fourteen component stylesheets carry a CIRCULAR CITATION banner and cursor-glow is not among them — the word in its file is the CSS keyword circle, in radial-gradient(520px circle at ...).

The debt that is real is narrower: the ledger says the verbatim source-diff against brittanychiang was never pinned ("well-known pattern"). So putting the glow on more pages does not spread an unsourced element; it spreads a Rod-authorised Remixed one whose diff is still owed. This page is a tuning bench and does not decide that either way.

notes

Provenance (report only). cursor-glow is Remixed / theirs — brittanychiang + merodev, Rod-authorised not-slop 2026-06-09, element-tracker.md:45. It does not carry a CIRCULAR CITATION banner; the 14 that do are listed in docs/MERGE-WORKLIST.md item 16 and this is not one of them. The open debt is that the verbatim source-diff was never pinned. A tuning page is not a shipping decision.

Readouts. "measured" means read back off the rendered element with getComputedStyle. "dial" means it is the number in the code, not a measurement of the drawn pixels — the tip spikes are canvas strokes and are not measurable without sampling the canvas.

Colour pickup. While the cursor is over this panel, mouse-trail.js:328 samples the panel's gold, not the stage's. Judge colour over the stage.

Two known limits of the live patch. The pool is allocated once at a fixed ceiling so the "sparks alive at once" dial can move without going out of bounds; and lowering that dial can strand a few already-live sparks, which reappear if it is raised again. Neither affects the numbers you copy out.
