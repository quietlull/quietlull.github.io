Paper tuner

option E over the LIVE three.js scene · lab · all options
The real scene is running behind this page, not the bloom stand-in - that is the point. Paper that reads on a flat panel can vanish over a live gradient.

Starting values are option E as the scene ships it: washi at tile 1 (256px) carrying the large fibre, cold press at tile 4 (64px) inside it as tooth, combined with a subtract-like blend. Sheets are the same baked files the shader loads.

The gradient map is a real lerp now. out = lerp(A, B, t), where t is the paper's own value - A lands on the troughs, B on the peaks. The first version laid a spatial gradient over the texture and blended it, which colours by POSITION rather than by VALUE; that was not a gradient map and it read as a glow.

Scale the noise down with AMOUNT, which fades the sheet layer only, or with the two TILE sliders, which change its frequency. They are separate on purpose.
