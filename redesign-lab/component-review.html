THREE THINGS I GOT WRONG THAT THE BRIEFS CAUGHT. Listed up front because they change specs I had already given you.

1. The related-card "exactly three deletions" spec was wrong. I wrote it from a grep. The hover rule I told you to delete is a suppressor — deleting it adds a 24px gold bloom. 2. The heading anchor's scroll-margin-top: 6.6rem is calibrated to Maxime's nav, not ours. At ≤780px it leaves -2px and at ≤560px -18px — the heading tucks under our sticky bar. Derived from the real bar tokens now.

Maxime's --anchor-border-color was exactly the cancel for this; my port kept the half that was fighting nothing and dropped the half that mattered.

The finding that reframes the whole thing: Minecraft's unearned state is not dimmed, faded or hollow — it is a different HUE at full saturation and near-identical luminance.
