Placing windows by hand

Drag a window in the page and let it settle - the page's own drag does the moving, this panel just writes down where you left it, and the copy-out turns that into that window's own data-x / data-y. Throw one and it records where it comes to REST, not where you let go. The title bar carries the grab cursor; with a mouse the whole window drags. Arrow keys move the window you last touched by 1px, or 10 with shift.

How big the windows are

PREVIEW IS APPROXIMATE. The engine still grows any window whose own title would not fit, and still forces the two lead doors taller than the tallest social - neither floor is applied here. The pasted data-w / data-h get both.

THE PREVIEW COUNTS A SIZE DIAL TWICE ON THE WIDTH, and the file will only count it once. Height is faithful; it is width that doubles. Judge the numbers, not the picture, when you change a size. Measured: the door dial at 1.30x draws Tech Art at 394px where its own numbers say 300. The engine sizes a window to fit its title by reading title.scrollWidth, and the title is a flex:1 1 auto child - so when the text fits, that reads back the box it has been GIVEN rather than the width the word needs. The dial widens the box, the engine measures the widened box and adopts it as the window's size, then the dial multiplies that again. It settles rather than running away (the engine restarts from the authored size every relayout), and it is not new - it is exactly what the two group dials have always done. Nothing has been changed to work around it: that would be a pixel change to the picture you judge by. its size on screen below prints what is drawn and what the paste will actually give, side by side, whenever the two disagree.

Sizing one window on its own

These follow the window you last touched - drag one, or just press it, and it becomes the one they size. Every other window is left alone. They sit ON TOP of the two group dials above: the group scales all three doors or all five socials, this multiplies that one window again, so 1.00 here means "whatever the group says" and the group dial still moves this window with the rest.

A resize does NOT move the window, and the readout shows both halves of that. What is held is its place as a proportion of the area - which is exactly what data-x means, so a growing window's left edge slides left while it stays in the same relative spot. The NUMBER still changes, because data-x is a fraction of (500 - data-w) and a wider window has a shorter run to travel - which is why data-x / data-y are always emitted next to data-w / data-h. Pasting a new size on its own is the thing that would move it.
