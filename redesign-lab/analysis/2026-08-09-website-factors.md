## REVISED FACTOR SET (2026-08-09, Rod)
**Some factors can't be judged from static screenshots** — I can't *see* motion, *feel* it, or *interact*. So:
- **AI-analyzable (visible) factors — use these for tagging + best-in-class:** LAYOUT · COLOR · TYPE · IMAGERY · NAV · ATMOSPHERE · IDENTITY. **Identity is an umbrella** — it absorbs the old *texture*, *craft*, and *personality* factors (and overlaps atmosphere).
- **Rod-only factors (NOT AI-judged):** MOTION and INTERACTIVITY. Carry Rod's stated notes from his gallery ratings as-is (e.g., unseen S+ interaction, fumino/jaimekim line-boil); don't rate them independently.

The 12-factor breakdown below stays as reference/context, but the loop tags + judges only the 7 visible factors.

## THE FACTORS (full reference list — analyzed as a spectrum: Rod-vibe end ↔ not-Rod end)

1. **Layout & composition** — rigid grid ↔ organic/scattered · cards ↔ full-bleed bands ↔ explorable scene · density ↔ negative space · symmetric ↔ asymmetric · bento / masonry · section rhythm.
2. **Color & palette** — light ↔ dark ground · warm ↔ cool · mono ↔ multi · single-accent ↔ many-accent · gradient use · saturation.
3. **Typography** — display voice (hand-drawn/brush ↔ serif ↔ grotesque ↔ mono) · body font · scale/contrast · tracking · tategaki/vertical · type-as-hero.
4. **Imagery / illustration / media** — photography ↔ illustration ↔ 3D-WebGL ↔ video · hand-painted ↔ rendered · art direction · asset quality.
5. **Motion & animation** — ambient/idle motion · entrance/scroll reveals · easing character (smooth ↔ springy/bouncy) · page transitions · drift/float · "line boil" / frame-rate feel.
6. **Interactivity** — custom cursor / trails / spotlight · 3D interactables & toys · reactive scenes (mouse-reactive water) · hover micro-interactions · feedback "feel" · games / easter eggs.
7. **Atmosphere & mood** — warm-cozy ↔ cold-premium · playful ↔ serious · wonder/whimsy · "internet"-ness · homemade ↔ polished · calm ↔ energetic.
8. **Personality & voice** — microcopy tone · hidden surprises · mascots/companions · feature density · presence widgets (now-playing, status) · self-expression ↔ professional.
9. **Navigation & IA** — nav style (top/side/pill/text) · entry gates (Enter screens) · work presentation (grid/list/scene/scroll) · scannability.
10. **Surface & texture** — grain/paper/washi ↔ glass/blur ↔ flat · glow/light (pooled ↔ spread) · depth/parallax.
11. **Identity & branding** — logo style (hand-drawn/seal/wordmark) · favicon craft · signature motif · mark-derived-from-artwork.
12. **Craft & cohesion** — detail · consistency · "objectively good" execution ↔ rough-but-charming (Rod often *prefers* rough-but-charming — flag when the two diverge).
