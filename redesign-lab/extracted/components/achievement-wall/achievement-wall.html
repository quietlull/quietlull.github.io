What is real and what is not

  • The 29 achievements are real, transcribed from _javascript/modules/components/achievements.js. Category counts match the blockout exactly: explorer 7, reader 6, interactor 8, secret 3, meta 5.
  • Unlock state and progress are demo values. The real ones are per visitor and live in the browser's own storage.
  • "Active" is a genuinely new thing. Your data knows whether an achievement is earned; it has nothing for whether it is switched on. Those are different.
  • There is no scene hook and nothing pretends there is one. No stub, no comment promising one. Writing one now would be inventing a contract that has not been decided.

Three things you should see rather than be told

  • The beetle is an empty box. Look at Interactor, the fifth tile. That is "Hey, You Touched My Fly!" and your Windows font has no glyph for it, so it renders as tofu. It is locked here so it is dimmed, but the missing box is visible.
  • Two tiles are identical. "First Light" (Explorer, first) and "Lantern Tapper" (Interactor) both use the same lantern emoji. On an icon-only wall there is nothing to tell them apart but the tooltip.
  • Progress does not exist for most of them. Only 11 of 29 carry a real number; the rest are yes/no. Those say Earned or Not yet earned instead of inventing a scale.

Choices already made, and how to see the alternative

  • Panel is 360 tall, not the blockout's 250 - your call. Flip old 250px panel to see why: it was reserved for an unlabelled box, and five labelled fields overflow it the moment Flavor and Effect have real words in them.
  • The rim keeps the project cards' own 220px reach - your call, no retune. Because that is more than three times a tile's width, it reads as a soft all-over glow rather than the band travelling along an edge that you see on the big cards.
  • The rim lights from cursor proximity, which is how the project cards behave: tiles glow when the pointer is merely near them. Flip rim only on hover for the alternative, which is closer to the original source and is what a keyboard can actually see.
  • Locked tiles are genuinely disabled, not just dimmed - a pressable locked achievement would be a fake. The cost is that they leave the tab order: 15 of 29 today.
