Bio block

3 versions · bio_intro + bio_more · drawn inside the measured 675x112 / 675x138 reservation
The dashed gold rectangle is the reservation at true size. Measured off about-blockout.html?v=panels at viewport 1440 with getBoundingClientRect: bio_intro 675 x 112, bio_more 675 x 138, 10px apart. The blockout's own labels say 140 and 180; it renders 112 and 138, so the rendered value is used.

THE COPY DOES NOT FIT, AND THAT IS THE FIRST THING TO LOOK AT. The blockout drew bio_intro as six 1px schematic rules. The real bio_intro is a heading plus three paragraphs, which set ten lines at the decided 18px/130%. The exact overrun is printed beside each block below and is measured live on load, not typed in.

Nothing here shrinks the type or cuts his words to make the number work. Rod already rejected small type on this page once ("Text is too small... should try to be consistent with posts"), so the honest options are: grow the reservation, cut the copy, or split the block. His call, not mine.

Copy is verbatim from tech-art/about.md front matter. One mechanical change: his two em dashes are commas, per the no-em-dash house rule.

V1 · Panel - the block is a translucent box, the heading is plain inside

SOURCE dimden.dev .box, main.css?9 lines 168-180, read from source and transcribed in sources/section-and-heading-blocks.md section B. 17 of them on their homepage. TIER: Remixed · IDEA-ORIGIN theirs.
Verbatim: 2px border, its #383838, padding 10px 15px. Ours: radius 4->0 (shape rule), fill #000000a8->--color-panel (their translucency is the precedent, the warm grey is Rod's), 5px margin->the blockout's 10px gap.
The only translucent fill in that whole source note, which is the deciding property on a page whose ground is a live scene. Rod's own gallery note on this exact page: "stacked bordered panels ... personality through framing rather than typography."

Hey There, I'm Rod

I'm a technical artist focused on bridging the gap between art and engineering. I build shaders, tools, and rendering pipelines that help artists work faster and create more expressive visuals.

I started game dev in 2023 at Vancouver Film School and have been deep in the weeds of real-time rendering ever since. From reverse-engineering production shaders to building automated sprite baking pipelines, I love the puzzle of making complex visual systems accessible.

My toolkit spans HLSL/GLSL shaders, Blender Python scripting, Unreal materials, and custom tool development. I believe the best technical art is invisible, it just makes everything look better.

V2 · Heading card - the heading is the box, the prose sits bare under it

SOURCE cyanilux h2, h3, h4, style.css?v=12 lines 817-832, read from source and transcribed in sources/section-and-heading-blocks.md section A. 20 h2 on one page of theirs. TIER: Remixed · IDEA-ORIGIN theirs.
Verbatim: padding 7px 20px, the 10px solid left edge, and the idea that h2/h3/h4 all get the SAME box so no size escalation carries hierarchy. Ours: radius 7->0, accent #00aabb->the ladder colour of the level (palette law: no cool accent outside the sky), opaque #282828->--color-panel.
Could not transfer: their margin-top:30px above the heading - the head is always its block's first child here, so it never fires. Dropped rather than kept as a dead rule. Their nesting-by-fill (#282828->#303030) is also not taken: it keys off containment and these two blocks are siblings.

V3 · Bare - no box at all, one rule between the blocks

SOURCE iquilezles.org/style.css?v=2, transcribed in sources/section-and-heading-blocks.md section E and sources/iquilezles-prose.md. TIER: Remixed · IDEA-ORIGIN theirs.
Verbatim: the principle, quoted from the held note - "no boxes, borders, backgrounds, radii, shadows or padding are ever used to separate a component from prose". The section break is a bare <hr>: there is no hr{} rule in their stylesheet (grep returns 0), so there is none here either. That absence is the transcription.
Ours, and it is an inversion: their heading is pure white over #c0c0c0 body text; ours recedes because decisions.css makes body copy the brightest thing on the page. Mechanism kept, direction flipped.
Why it is in the set: the source note's own words - "the floor of the range. Included so the boxed options have something to be judged against; without it every variant on the page is a box and the comparison is rigged."
