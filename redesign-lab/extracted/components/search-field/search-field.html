Search field

Six candidates, each built from CSS read off the live site on 2026-08-24. Verbatim sources in sources/search-fields-2026-08-24.md. Each sits inside its two real reservations, drawn as dashed outlines: 1134×50 from the projects page and 340×47 from ramblings, both measured off the rendered pages at 1440.

Read this before judging. Of all 102 sites in the gallery, exactly two have an always-open inline search, and both are unstyled browser defaults. Everything else hides behind a toggle, a modal, a curtain or a keyboard shortcut. Our slot is an always-open box, so A is the only strict drop-in and it supplies no look at all. Six further candidates were killed: stripe, thatskygame and unseen have no search UI at all; Aman's field is stock Drupal CSS; The Point's is a Codrops widget, not their work.

tier ?

https://cinra.co.jp/ · inline <style>, 1,027 rules · modal

The border exists at rest but matches the fill, so focus costs zero layout: nothing grows, nothing reflows, the edge simply becomes visible. Click into it.

Their trick depends on an opaque fill and ours is over the scene, so it is re-derived with the resting border set to the fill's own colour. Their 4px radius is squared.

E · Craig Mod

tier ? · CUT

https://craigmod.com/css/master-1140.css · Cmd + / only, no visible trigger

Input and results are one continuous slab, which is why its top corners are round and its bottom ones are not. The only candidate in the gallery shipping a prefers-reduced-motion block.

Squaring it per the house rule costs the slab reading, which was the idea. Its backdrop-filter: blur(3px) is not carried (the glass tell), nor is its <mark> blue (a cool accent outside the sky).
