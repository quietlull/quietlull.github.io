Use headphones if you have them, and set the volume once at the start.

Each round plays two clips of typing, A and B. Play both, flip between
them as much as you want, and pick the one you like the sound of more. There are no right answers
and nothing is labelled. It takes about ten minutes, and your ranking shows up at the end.
