Design Direction Previews

Three takes on the Shinsekai Yori / Monogatari brief — all dark theme, all using your existing amber/gold palette. Lanterns reinterpreted as abstract light against dark, no CJK characters.

01 · Soft · Shinsekai-leaning

Drifting glow field

Atmospheric, contemplative, painterly. Soft amber glows drift in the background; subtle leaf silhouettes pass through. Cards have radial-masked edges that fade into the field rather than hard borders. Heavy negative space, Cormorant italic typography, slow rhythm.

Cormorant Garamond · Inter · JetBrains Mono
02 · Bold · Monogatari-leaning

Flat blocks and hard light

High-contrast, asymmetric, graphic. Pure black background with crisp pulsing amber points (no blur). Hero splits into two flat color blocks, project grid uses an asymmetric 2:1:1 layout with a feature card spanning two rows. Archivo Black for headlines, numbered sections, hard borders.

Archivo Black · Inter · JetBrains Mono
03 · Hybrid · Both

Soft field, one bold move

Atmospheric base of soft drifting amber glows (Shinsekai influence) under bold display typography mixing Archivo Black with italic Cormorant (Monogatari influence). Cards retain a soft backdrop-blur frame but get a small solid gold corner mark to anchor each one.

Archivo Black + Cormorant · Inter · JetBrains Mono
