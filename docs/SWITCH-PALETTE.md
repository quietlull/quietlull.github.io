# Switch palette for the sound tier list

The switches the tier test at `/norobots/sound-tiers-3c91d2e4/` will use, and where each sound clip
comes from. Built 2026-10-16 from the full Click and Thock channel list (643 videos, read with
yt-dlp). Rod's rules: every switch is on Click and Thock, so every clip is recorded the same way;
each family has two switches that are only that sound, plus a bridge that sits between two families
and counts for both. Click and Thock allows reuse of the audio (Rod, 2026-10-16).

**Take every clip from the same keycap set.** His reviews type each switch with three sets (GMK or
Cherry, MT3, SA). MT3 appears in both his older and newer format, so use MT3 throughout (inference:
the sets change the sound more than some switches differ).

**"His words"** is the sound description in the video title or on his site. Where the family is my
reading rather than his word, the row says so.

## What was left out, and why

- **Rainy is folded in, not a family.** His three "rain" switches are described by him as marbly
  (Kailh Rainy Whisper), creamy-marbly (Jerrzi Rain Bow) and creamy (Jerrzi Wind Rain), so the first
  two sit in Marbly and Creamy. "Rainy" elsewhere means one keyboard, the Wobkey Rainy 75.
- **Muted has one pure switch.** He uses the word only in site copy; U4Tx ("half-thock") is the one
  title that says it. The two bridges carry the rest.
- **No Cherry switches** (Rod: decent quality only).
- **Alps on vintage boards.** Only the Tofu60 section of the SKCM White video matches the modern
  boards the rest are typed on.
