# Design pillars (the razor) — v2 (2026-08-08)

The clean pillars list [[project-design-vision]] implied but never enumerated. Synthesized from Rod's 82-site gallery ratings, then made **actionable** + grouped into the **3 axes Rod recognized** (he saw 1+2, 4+5, 3+6 as connected). Use like the impeccable fork ([[project-impeccable-adoption]]): backed by a pillar → keep (ship the elevated version); backed by none → cut. Still iterating with Rod.

**North star (unchanged):** "Look at this, not look at me." Point at the work / a cool thing, casual-excited like showing a friend. Never at Rod's status.

---

## AXIS I — WARM & HAND-MADE
*A person made this, and they're warm.* (pillars: warm-not-cold-corporate + hand-crafted-not-sterile — **Rod named the cold-corporate one himself**)
**Do:**
- Logos & section headers **hand-drawn / painted / brush**, not geometric wordmarks.
- **Warm amber/gold pooled light** on dark; never neutral-gray "premium."
- **Visible texture** — washi/paper, grain, watercolor, line-boil (→ paper-morphism over glass).
- **Microcopy with voice**; never corporate taglines or "trying to look serious."
**Cut if:** cold · corporate · premium-quiet · sterile-flat.
**Evidence:** cut Death & Co / Aman / A24 / Craig Mod / RSW / Mahora / Tawaraya; harumaki **S**, fumino/pomodorosa **B+**, jaimekim.

## AXIS II — ALIVE & PLAYFUL
*It moves, it reacts, it's fun to poke.* (pillars: alive/interactive + "internet"/playful)
**Do:**
- **Interactions that "feel nice" are REQUIRED, not garnish** — hover behaviors, floaty/springy/magnetic motion, satisfying micro-reactions. An element can earn its place on interaction ALONE (helloteajp went F→**C** purely for hover).
- **3D / interactable toys** — draggable objects, your Three.js scenes (109ichiki **A**).
- **Hidden surprises + feature density** — cursor companions, toggles, dimden-style feature-slop.
- **"Screams internet"** personality over polished-SaaS.
**Cut if:** static-even-if-pretty · dead/janky interaction.
**Interaction &ne; exploration (LOCKED 2026-08-09).** Aliveness comes from MICRO-interactions on a normal page &mdash; interesting buttons, fun elements, shader effects &mdash; NOT spatial/navigational exploration. Rod explicitly does NOT want "explore the ruins / dive into the sea / click the market stalls" travel-between-places. &rarr; the unseen **S+** steal is the *reactive-water EFFECT* (ripple/glow on mouse), NOT its dive-underwater navigation.
**Watch (Rod's anti-patterns):** no intrusive center-screen popups · don't over-animate the background into distraction · keep asset/image quality high. (All three from the thatskygame critique.)
**Evidence:** **unseen.co S+ = the Axis-II north star + STEAL TARGET** (a 3D scene reacting to the mouse: wavy water that ripples/glows on touch, a dive-underwater page transition with distortion, cursor-trailing butterflies — ties DIRECTLY to Rod's water-scene track; links to more S-tier interaction demos: hubtown.co.in/projects, experiencethebestyou.com, unseen.co/labs/blossom), dimden **S**, 109ichiki **A**, kaitonote **B**, helloteajp **C** (interactions alone), merodev **A**.

## AXIS III — OPEN & WONDROUS
*You arrive somewhere with room to breathe and a sense of wonder.* (pillars: open/airy + wonder/festival)
**Do:**
- **One clear scene that breathes** — generous, uneven, intentional negative space. Never cluttered.
- **Environment-forward landing** — arrive INTO a place/scene, not a UI.
- **Split the page into "zones" with ORGANIC partitions** (clouds / noise / ink / mist), not hard lines. *(Rod loved this in Sky/thatskygame.)*
- **The festival/lantern/night-market soul** — glowing warm night scenes, wonder; the genuine Zoe-festival identity.
**Cut if:** cluttered/busy · generic.
**"Open" = openly laid out + genuinely easy to navigate (conventional, scannable) &mdash; NOT an explorable world (LOCKED 2026-08-09).** The festival/lantern/night-market is ATMOSPHERE & aesthetic, NOT a navigation metaphor &mdash; Rod does not want to "wander" spatially. An atmospheric immersive *landing* (a scene you arrive at) + organic zone-partitions on a normal scrolling page are fine; place-to-place travel is not. (This supersedes the old "night market you wander" organizing-metaphor idea in [[project-visual-references]] &mdash; keep the aesthetic, drop the wander.)
**Evidence:** harumaki **S** (open beats TUYU/ZUTOMAYO's clutter → both **B**), thatskygame **A-**, hoshimachi **A-** (open space), jaimekim, Sky/Spiritfarer art.

## CROSS-CUTTING — TYPE & SPACE
*Shows up in nearly every rating Rod wrote* ("good fonts," "good/great spacing," "use of space").
**Do:**
- **Distinctive, characterful type** (a hand-drawn display + a clean characterful body/mono). NEVER Inter / generic default.
- **Intentional, uneven, generous spacing** — hand-placed, not an algorithmic grid.

**Display voice LOCKED (2026-08-09):** **scribbly / messy hand-drawn FONT + line-boil**, used for the **name / logo card** especially. Support = **clean mono** (109ichiki register). **NOT serif / fancy / elegant** &mdash; Rod: "I'm not that fancy or elegant... a fairly simple, messy person." Crucial: the hand-drawn look comes from **fonts + effects (line-boil), NOT Rod drawing it himself** (he says he can't draw well) &mdash; so lean on scribble-style typefaces + procedural line-boil, not hand illustration. Reinforces intentionally-rough > polished (see Axis-adjacent "Craft" note).
**Evidence:** "good fonts" (109ichiki, sengoku, potg, hoshimachi, thatskygame); "good/great spacing" (harumaki, potg, zutomayo, hoshimachi).

cold/corporate · cluttered/busy · static-even-if-pretty · generic/not-distinctive · trying-to-look-serious · **intrusive center popups** · **distracting over-animated background** · **low asset quality**.
