Rod's rules for ANY content/writing (blog posts, site copy, anything I draft or edit for him):

**No em dashes, ever.** Use commas (or periods/colons) instead. He considers the em dash a clear AI tell that has "poisoned its usage everywhere." Applies to content I WRITE and content I EDIT for him. Also avoid them in chat as a courtesy.

**Do not formalize or sanitize his voice.** When he asks me to proofread or tighten, fix ONLY mechanical errors (typos, spelling, subject/verb agreement, comma splices, fragments, hyphens) and cut genuine redundancy. Do NOT rewrite his phrasings into cleaner/more formal prose. He explicitly rejected a pass where I "removed a lot of the character" and made it "more formal." His voice is casual, enthusiastic, first-person, lowercase-and-loose, "look at this cool thing" energy (matches [[project_design_vision]] "look at this not look at me").

**Why:** he wants his writing to sound like HIM, not a polished AI.
**How to apply:** light-touch proofread; preserve his register and word choices; replace any em dash with a comma; present a cleaned copy he can paste, plus a short list of the mechanical fixes so he can see exactly what changed.
