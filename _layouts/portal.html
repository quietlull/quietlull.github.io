It is a viewport-locked field of draggable windows: sizes and positions come from spacejam.com/1996 (D15), the frame ratios from zutomayo's served CSS, and the drag, throw, bounce and lean are ours. Full provenance lives in `_sass/components/_portal-window.scss` and `assets/js/effects/portal-windows.js`. It carries no top bar and no footer (Rod, 2026-08-23) and it does not scroll - `default.html` handles both. `data-x/y/w/h` are Space Jam's measurements in its own 500x520 stage, not pixels on this page; the JS rescales them. Do not correct them against what you measure here. {% endcomment %}
